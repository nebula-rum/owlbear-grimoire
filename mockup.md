# The Sundered Grimoire

*A player-facing reference for the Ashfall campaign — upload this file to Drive, share it "Anyone with the link," and add it to your vault as a Markdown item to see it rendered exactly like this inside the viewer.*

Once bound to a caster, the Grimoire cannot be destroyed by conventional means — only **unbound**, and only by the ritual described below. Its pages rearrange themselves to show whatever the reader most needs, which is either a blessing or a curse depending on how you look at it.

## Casting Basics

Every spell cast from the Grimoire costs **Focus**, tracked on your character sheet. You regain Focus on a short rest (half your maximum, rounded up) or a long rest (full).

1. Declare the spell and its tier.
2. Spend Focus equal to the tier (see table below).
3. Roll `2d6 + Casting` against the listed difficulty.
4. On a **failure**, the Grimoire exacts a *toll* — roll on the Backlash table at the GM's discretion.

> **GM tip:** Don't telegraph the backlash roll. Let players commit to the spell before they know what it costs them — that tension is the whole point of a bound grimoire instead of a clean spell list.

### Inline notes

Some notation you'll see throughout: `ADV` means roll twice and keep the higher result, `DIS` means keep the lower. Damage in *italics* is elemental and bypasses mundane resistance. ~~Struck-through~~ spells have been removed from this printing — ask your GM before using them anyway.

## Spell Tiers

| Tier | Focus Cost | Difficulty | Example |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| I — Cantrip | 0 | 6 | Light, Mend, Whisper |
| II — Minor | 1 | 8 | Firebolt, Shield, Charm |
| III — Lesser | 2 | 10 | Fireball, Haste, Banish |
| IV — Greater | 4 | 13 | Chain Lightning, Wall of Stone |
| V — Grimoire-bound | 7 | 16 | Only castable *from this book* |

Tier V spells require the Grimoire itself to be open on the table (or, for our purposes, presented to everyone via the GM's 📣 button) — the ritual doesn't work if only the caster can see the pages.

## The Unbinding Ritual

```
Ritual: Sever the Binding
Casting time: 1 hour, uninterrupted
Components: the Grimoire, a blade that has drawn the caster's blood,
            and one truth the caster has never spoken aloud
Effect: the Grimoire goes dormant for 1d4 days, during which it can
        be rebound to a new caster or safely destroyed.
```

Interrupting the ritual before the final hour has a **60% chance** of the backlash rebounding onto everyone within `30 ft`, not just the caster — plan your battle map accordingly.
